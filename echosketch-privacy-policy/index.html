Privacy Policy

Avi Bortnick built EchoSketch as a commercial music audio plugin and standalone app. It processes audio on your device to create delay and reverb effects. This page explains our approach to your privacy.

Data Collection & Privacy

EchoSketch is a "privacy-first" app.

  • No data collected: The app does not require a login, an account, or an internet connection. We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, or any personal information.
  • No tracking: We do not track or transmit your presets, audio, settings, or usage in any way.
  • Everything stays local: Your presets and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Data deletion: Because we never collect or store your data on any server, there is nothing for us to delete. Removing the app removes its data from your device.

Children's Privacy

EchoSketch does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
