OverScores

iPhone / iPad / Mac App

Full-featured iOS & Mac sheet music reader, library and PDF markup app with unique features for memorization and score clean-up.

Key Features

  • Hiding of measures and staves to foster memorization
  • Clean-up and straightening of staves from bowed pages (from scans of an open book, for example)
  • MusicXML file chord extract and transposition
  • Auto-detection of measures
  • Practice time logging
  • Audio recording
  • Streaming links
  • Option to split large PDF compendiums into component pieces

Everywhere you play

  • Universal — iPad, iPhone, and Mac, one purchase
  • Edits on the Mac version can transfer to iPad and vice-versa via iCloud sync
