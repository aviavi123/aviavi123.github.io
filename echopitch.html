Echo Pitch

Mac / iOS Plugin

Pitch-shifting multi-delay and harmonizer for cascading delays and loops.

Echo Pitch is a VST3 and Audio Unit (AUv3) plugin that combines a flexible multi-tap delay with real-time pitch shifting. Each repeat can be transposed, so echoes cascade up or down in pitch — perfect for shimmering ambient textures, harmonized loops, and otherworldly delay trails inside your DAW on Mac or your favorite AUv3 host on iOS.

Key Features

  • Pitch-shifted feedback: Transpose each delay repeat to build cascading, evolving pitch trails.
  • Multi-delay engine: Layer multiple delay voices for rhythmic and ambient patterns.
  • Harmonizer mode: Add tuned intervals on top of your signal for instant harmonies.
  • Mac and iOS: Runs as a VST3 or AUv3 plugin on Mac, or standalone, and as an AUv3 plugin or standalone on iOS. (Purchase Mac version here; iOS version at App Store.)
