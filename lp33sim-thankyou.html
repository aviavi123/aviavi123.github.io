Installation

Standalone app and Audio Unit v3 plugin (Logic, GarageBand, etc.)

  1. Open LP-33 Sim Studio from your Applications folder once.
  2. Enter your email address and paste your license key (it begins with LP33).

That first launch registers the Audio Unit, so it appears in your AU hosts.

VST3 (Ableton Live and other VST3 hosts)

Rescan plug-ins in your host, then add LP-33 Sim. If you already entered your license in LP-33 Sim Studio, the VST3 will be unlocked too; otherwise enter it in the plug-in.
